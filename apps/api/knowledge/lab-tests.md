Knowing which tests answer which question helps you ask your doctor for the right ones.

## Tiredness, hair fall, breathlessness
- **CBC (complete blood count):** checks haemoglobin for anaemia.
- **Ferritin:** shows iron stores.
- **Vitamin B12 and vitamin D:** deficiencies are common in India.
- **TSH:** screens for thyroid problems.

## Irregular periods, acne, excess hair
- **Total testosterone:** high levels suggest high androgens, as in PCOS.
- **LH and FSH:** best done on day 2 to 5 of your cycle. Results depend on cycle timing.
- **Prolactin:** high levels can stop periods.
- **TSH:** thyroid problems can cause irregular cycles.
- **Fasting glucose or HbA1c:** PCOS raises the risk of diabetes.
- **Pelvic ultrasound:** looks at the ovaries and uterus.

## Reading your report
Each lab prints its own reference range next to the result. Always compare with that range, because ranges differ between labs and units. A result slightly outside the range isn't always a problem, so talk it through with your doctor.
