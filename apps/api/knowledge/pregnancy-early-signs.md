A missed period is often the first sign of pregnancy. Other early signs include tender breasts, nausea, tiredness and needing to pee more often.

## Testing
Home urine pregnancy tests are reliable from the first day of a missed period. If it is negative but your period still doesn't come, test again after a few days.

## Ectopic pregnancy: know the warning signs
An ectopic pregnancy grows outside the uterus, usually in a fallopian tube, and can be life-threatening. Get emergency care (108 or 112) if you might be pregnant and have:
- Pain low in the belly on one side
- Vaginal bleeding or brown watery discharge
- Pain in the tip of your shoulder
- Dizziness, fainting or feeling very unwell

## Planning a pregnancy
Start folic acid before trying to conceive, check your haemoglobin, thyroid and blood sugar, and track your cycle to understand your fertile window.
