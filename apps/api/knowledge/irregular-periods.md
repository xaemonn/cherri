Periods are irregular when the time between them keeps changing a lot, or when they come less often than every 35 days. Going 3 months or more without a period (when not pregnant) is called amenorrhoea and should always be checked.

## Common causes
- Pregnancy (always rule this out first)
- Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
- Thyroid problems
- High prolactin
- Stress, big weight changes, very intense exercise or eating too little
- Puberty, breastfeeding and perimenopause
- Some contraceptives

## When to see a doctor
See a doctor if your periods suddenly become irregular, come less than 21 days apart, are more than 35 days apart for several cycles, or stop for 3 months. Bring your logged cycle dates: they make diagnosis much faster.
