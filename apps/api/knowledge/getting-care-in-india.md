Good care doesn't have to be expensive. These options are free or low-cost.

## eSanjeevani
The Government of India's free telemedicine service. You can consult a doctor online from your phone, and it is useful for a first opinion or a follow-up.

## Government health facilities
Primary and community health centres (PHCs and CHCs) and district hospitals offer free consultations, free iron-folic acid tablets, contraception and many basic tests.

## Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
Eligible families get free treatment at empanelled public and private hospitals. You can check eligibility and find hospitals on the PM-JAY website.

## Which doctor?
- Periods, pain, PCOS, fertility or contraception: a gynaecologist
- Thyroid or diabetes: a physician or endocrinologist
- Mood and mental health: a psychiatrist or counsellor (Tele-MANAS 14416 is free)
- Skin and hair changes: a dermatologist, and also check hormones with a gynaecologist
