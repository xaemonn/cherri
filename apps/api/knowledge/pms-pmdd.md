Premenstrual syndrome (PMS) is the physical and emotional symptoms many people get in the 1 to 2 weeks before their period: mood swings, irritability, tiredness, bloating, breast tenderness, cravings and headaches. Symptoms ease once the period starts.

## PMDD
Premenstrual dysphoric disorder (PMDD) is a severe form of PMS. Mood symptoms such as depression, hopelessness, anxiety, anger or feeling out of control become strong enough to disrupt work, studies or relationships. It is a recognised medical condition, not a weakness.

## Diagnosis
Doctors look for symptoms that appear in the week before the period, improve within a few days after it starts and are minimal in the week after. Daily tracking for at least 2 cycles is needed to confirm the pattern.

## What helps
Regular exercise, good sleep, cutting down on caffeine and alcohol, and talking therapies such as CBT. Doctors may prescribe antidepressants (SSRIs), sometimes only in the second half of the cycle, or hormonal treatments.
