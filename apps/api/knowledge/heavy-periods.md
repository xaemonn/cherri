Heavy menstrual bleeding is common and treatable, but it is often ignored. It is one of the main causes of iron-deficiency anaemia in women.

## Signs your periods are heavy
- Soaking through a pad or tampon every 1 to 2 hours
- Needing to change protection during the night, or using two pads together
- Passing blood clots larger than about 2.5 cm (a large coin)
- Bleeding for more than 7 days
- Feeling tired or breathless during or after your period

## Possible causes
Fibroids, polyps, adenomyosis, endometriosis, PCOS, thyroid problems, bleeding disorders and some contraceptives such as the copper IUD. Often no single cause is found, and treatment still helps.

## What to do
See a doctor. Ask for a haemoglobin and ferritin test to check for anaemia. Treatments include tranexamic acid, hormonal options such as the hormonal IUD, and treating any underlying cause. If you are soaking a pad every hour and feel dizzy or faint, seek care urgently.
