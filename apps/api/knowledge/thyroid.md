The thyroid gland in your neck makes hormones that control your energy use. Thyroid problems are common in women and can affect periods, mood, weight and fertility.

## Underactive thyroid (hypothyroidism)
Tiredness, weight gain, feeling cold, constipation, dry skin, hair thinning, low mood, and heavier or irregular periods.

## Overactive thyroid (hyperthyroidism)
Racing heart, anxiety, shaky hands, feeling hot and sweaty, weight loss, and lighter or less frequent periods.

## Tests
A TSH blood test is the first step. High TSH usually means an underactive thyroid and low TSH an overactive one. Doctors may add free T4 and thyroid antibody tests.

## Treatment
Underactive thyroid is treated with a daily levothyroxine tablet, with the dose adjusted using regular blood tests. Overactive thyroid has several treatments. With treatment, most people feel much better and periods often settle.
