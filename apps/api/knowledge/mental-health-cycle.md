Hormone changes across the cycle can affect mood, sleep and anxiety. Noticing a pattern helps you plan, and helps a doctor tell PMS or PMDD apart from depression or anxiety that doesn't follow your cycle.

## Small things that help
Regular sleep, daily movement, sunlight, regular meals, staying connected with people you trust, and cutting back on caffeine in the week before your period.

## When to get help
Talk to a doctor or counsellor if low mood, anxiety or irritability last most days for 2 weeks or more, affect your studies, work or relationships, or come back strongly every cycle.

## Free support
Tele-MANAS, the Government of India's mental health helpline, is free and open 24×7 on 14416 or 1-800-891-4416. If you are in immediate danger, call 112.
