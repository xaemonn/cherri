Food alone may not fix established anaemia, but eating iron-rich foods every day helps prevent it and supports treatment.

## Vegetarian sources
Green leafy vegetables (palak, methi, bathua, sarson, amaranth), dals and legumes (rajma, chana, moong, masoor), ragi and bajra, poha (flattened rice), jaggery (gur), dates, raisins, sesame (til), garden cress seeds (halim) and nuts.

## Non-vegetarian sources
Red meat, liver, chicken, fish and eggs contain haem iron, which the body absorbs much more easily than iron from plants.

## Absorb more of it
- Add vitamin C to iron-rich meals: lemon, amla, guava, oranges, tomatoes.
- Keep tea and coffee at least an hour away from meals. They block iron absorption.
- Cooking in an iron kadhai can add a little iron to food.
- Sprouting and fermenting (sprouts, idli, dosa) make plant iron easier to absorb.

## Easy ideas
Palak dal with lemon, poha with peanuts and lemon, ragi dosa, chana chaat with tomato and lemon, til-gur laddoo, or sprouts salad with amla.
