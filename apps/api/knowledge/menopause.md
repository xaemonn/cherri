Menopause is when periods stop for good, confirmed after 12 months without a period. The years before it are called perimenopause. In India, menopause often happens a little earlier than the global average of about 51, commonly in the mid-to-late 40s.

## Perimenopause symptoms
Irregular periods, hot flushes, night sweats, poor sleep, mood changes, brain fog, vaginal dryness and joint aches. Symptoms can start years before periods stop.

## What helps
Regular exercise, a calcium- and protein-rich diet, good sleep, and limiting alcohol and caffeine. Hormone therapy (HRT) and non-hormonal treatments can help bothersome symptoms; discuss the benefits and risks with a doctor.

## Don't ignore
Any bleeding after menopause needs a prompt check-up. Menopause also raises the risk of osteoporosis and heart disease, so bone and heart health checks matter.
