There is no single best period product. Choose what is comfortable, affordable and safe for you.

## Options
- **Disposable pads:** easy to use. Change every 4 to 6 hours, or sooner on heavy days.
- **Reusable cloth pads:** cheaper and eco-friendly. Wash with soap and dry fully in sunlight.
- **Menstrual cups:** reusable for years and can usually be worn for up to 8 to 12 hours, depending on flow and the maker's advice. Wash between uses and boil between periods.
- **Tampons:** change every 4 to 8 hours and use the lowest absorbency you need.

## Toxic shock syndrome (TSS)
TSS is rare but serious, and is linked to leaving tampons in too long. Remove the tampon and seek urgent care if you suddenly get a high fever, rash, vomiting or dizziness.

## Good habits
Wash your hands before and after changing, wash the outer genital area with plain water (no douching or scented products), and wrap used products before throwing them away.
